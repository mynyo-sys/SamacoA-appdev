## Introduction

Welcome to the Samaco Brewery App! This mobile application allows you to browse, select, and order craft beers from Samaco Brewery directly from your smartphone. This guide will walk you through all the features and functionality of the app.

### What You Can Do
- Browse our selection of craft beers
- Filter products by category
- Search for specific products
- Add items to your shopping cart
- Place orders
- View your order history
- Manage your profile

## Getting Started

### System Requirements

**For Mobile App:**
- Android 5.0 (Lollipop) or higher
- iOS 12 or higher
- 100MB of free storage space
- Stable internet connection

**For Web Access:**
- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Stable internet connection

## Installation

### For Android Users

1. Download the APK file from the official Samaco Brewery website
2. Enable "Unknown Sources" in your phone settings:
   - Go to Settings > Security
   - Enable "Unknown Sources"
3. Open the downloaded APK file
4. Tap "Install"
5. Wait for installation to complete
6. Tap "Open" to launch the app

### For iOS Users

1. Open the App Store
2. Search for "Samaco Brewery"
3. Tap "Get" to download
4. Wait for installation to complete
5. Tap the app icon to launch

### For Web Users

1. Open your web browser
2. Navigate to: `https://webdev2-staging.up.railway.app`
3. Bookmark the page for easy access

## Account Setup

### Creating an Account

The app uses Google Sign-In for authentication. To create an account:

1. Launch the Samaco Brewery App
2. Tap the "Sign In with Google" button
3. Select your Google account
4. Grant permissions to the app
5. Your account will be automatically created

### First-Time Setup

After signing in for the first time:
1. Review your profile information
2. Your email will be pre-filled from your Google account
3. You can update your name and other details in the Profile section

## Using the App

### Home Screen

The home screen displays:
- **Navigation bar** at the top with menu options
- **Featured products** section
- **Quick access** to categories
- **Search bar** for finding products

### Browsing Products

1. Tap on "Products" from the menu
2. You'll see all available products displayed in a grid
3. Scroll down to view more products
4. Tap on any product to view details

### Filtering by Category

1. On the Products screen, you'll see category chips below the search bar
2. Categories include:
   - All
   - Local Craft Beers
   - Imported Beers
   - Filipino Lagers
   - Seasonal Specials
   - Non-Alcoholic
3. Tap on any category to filter products
4. The active category will be highlighted in yellow

### Searching for Products

1. Tap the search bar at the top of the Products screen
2. Type the name of the beer you're looking for
3. Results will appear in real-time as you type
4. Tap the "X" button to clear your search

### Viewing Product Details

1. Tap on any product card
2. A modal will appear showing:
   - Product name
   - Category
   - Description
   - Price
   - Stock availability
3. Tap "Add to Cart" to add the item to your cart
4. Tap the "X" button to close the modal

### Adding Items to Cart

1. Sign in to your account (required)
2. Browse products and tap on any item
3. In the product detail modal, tap "Add to Cart"
4. A success message will appear
5. The item will be added to your cart
6. Stock will be updated locally

### Viewing Your Cart

1. Tap on "Orders" from the menu
2. Your cart items will be displayed at the top
3. You'll see:
   - Number of items in cart
   - Total amount
   - List of items with quantities
4. Tap "Place Order" to checkout

### Placing an Order

1. Ensure you have items in your cart
2. Go to the Orders screen
3. Review your cart items
4. Tap "Place Order"
5. Your order will be created
6. Cart will be cleared
7. Order will appear in your order history

### Viewing Order History

1. Tap on "Orders" from the menu
2. Scroll down to see your past orders
3. Each order shows:
   - Order number
   - Date
   - Status (Pending, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled)
   - Items ordered
   - Total amount
4. Tap on an order to view details

### Canceling an Order

1. Go to the Orders screen
2. Find the order you want to cancel
3. Only "Pending" orders can be canceled
4. Tap "Cancel Order" button
5. Confirm the cancellation
6. Order status will change to "Cancelled"

### Managing Your Profile

1. Tap on "Profile" from the menu
2. You'll see:
   - Your profile picture (initials)
   - Your name
   - Email address
   - Account statistics:
     - Total Orders
     - Total Spent
3. Tap "Logout" to sign out of your account

## Features

### Product Categories

- **Local Craft Beers:** Beers brewed locally with unique flavors
- **Imported Beers:** Premium beers from around the world
- **Filipino Lagers:** Traditional Filipino beer selections
- **Seasonal Specials:** Limited edition seasonal brews
- **Non-Alcoholic:** Alcohol-free beverage options

### Stock Indicators

- **In Stock:** Product is available
- **Low Stock:** Limited quantity available (less than 10)
- **Out of Stock:** Product is currently unavailable

### Order Status

- **Pending:** Order has been placed but not yet processed
- **Confirmed:** Order has been confirmed by the brewery
- **Completed:** Order has been fulfilled
- **Cancelled:** Order has been canceled by the user

## Troubleshooting

### App Won't Open

**Solution:**
- Force close the app and reopen
- Restart your phone
- Check for app updates
- Reinstall the app if necessary

### Can't Sign In

**Solution:**
- Ensure you have a stable internet connection
- Check if Google Sign-In is enabled
- Clear app cache and data
- Try signing in with a different Google account

### Products Not Loading

**Solution:**
- Check your internet connection
- Pull down to refresh the screen
- Close and reopen the app
- Check if the backend server is online

### Can't Add to Cart

**Solution:**
- Ensure you're signed in
- Check if the product is in stock
- Restart the app
- Clear app cache

### Order Not Placing

**Solution:**
- Ensure you have items in your cart
- Check your internet connection
- Verify your account is active
- Try again after a few moments

### Cart Items Disappeared

**Solution:**
- Cart items are stored locally on your device
- Clearing app data will clear your cart
- Items are saved until you place an order
- Check if you accidentally cleared app data

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need to create an account to browse products?**
A: No, you can browse products without signing in. However, you need to sign in to add items to cart and place orders.

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**
A: Yes, we use secure payment processing and do not store your payment details on our servers.

**Q: Can I modify my order after placing it?**
A: Orders can only be canceled if they are still in "Pending" status. Once confirmed, modifications are not possible.

**Q: How long does delivery take?**
A: Delivery times vary based on your location. You'll receive an estimated delivery time when your order is confirmed.

**Q: What if a product is out of stock?**
A: Out of stock items cannot be added to cart. We'll notify you when the item becomes available again.

**Q: Can I order from multiple locations?**
A: Currently, orders can only be placed from a single location per order.

**Q: How do I contact customer support?**
A: You can reach our customer support through the app's help section or email us at support@samacobrewery.com.

**Q: Is there a minimum order amount?**
A: No, there is no minimum order amount.

**Q: Can I save items for later?**
A: Currently, items can only be saved in your cart. Wishlist functionality is coming soon.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: We accept credit/debit cards, GCash, and cash on delivery.
